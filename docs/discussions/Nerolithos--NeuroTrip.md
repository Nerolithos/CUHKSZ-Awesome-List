## English

**Title: NeuroTrip is listed in Awesome CUHKSZ — student project suggestions welcome**

I have added my project NeuroTrip to the Student Projects section of Awesome CUHKSZ, which I also maintain. NeuroTrip combines AI, information visualization, and interactive storytelling to explore themes involving perception, language, memory, and the brain.

Current listing description:

> Interactive neuroscience experience combining AI and information visualization.

If you are building a CUHK-Shenzhen-related interactive experience, visualization, or student project, suggestions through an issue or PR in the list repository are welcome. Corrections to NeuroTrip’s description are welcome too. New projects do not need a minimum star count; please disclose your relationship when submitting your own work.

[View the corresponding list section](https://github.com/Nerolithos/CUHKSZ-Awesome-List#student).

— Nerolithos
