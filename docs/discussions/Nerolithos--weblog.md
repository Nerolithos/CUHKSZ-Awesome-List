**Title: Collecting CUHK-Shenzhen campus and course resources in Awesome CUHKSZ**

Alongside this weblog, I am maintaining Awesome CUHKSZ, an index of campus tools, course resources, research projects, and student work related to CUHK-Shenzhen. The blog is listed under Useful Resources as a personal campus guide and course-explanation resource.

Current listing description:

> Personal campus guide and course explanations for CUHKSZ students.
