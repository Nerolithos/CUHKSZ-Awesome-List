# Community Code of Conduct

English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our commitment

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Treat people with respect, credit their work, and make room for beginners. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and interactions where someone represents this project.

## Expected behavior

Use considerate language, offer specific and constructive feedback, respect disagreement, disclose conflicts of interest, and protect privacy. Ask before publishing another person’s identity or private correspondence.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized comments, threats, doxxing, repeated unwanted contact, personal attacks, and promotional spam are unacceptable. Critique the work without attacking its author. Stars and school status do not confer authority over others.

## Reporting

Contact the repository owner privately through a contact method they explicitly publish on their GitHub profile. If no private method is available, open a minimal issue asking for a private reporting channel; include no names, allegations, or sensitive details. Do not post the report publicly. A maintainer involved in a report must recuse themselves; the reporter may request review by another uninvolved maintainer when one is available. During single-maintainer operation, independent review cannot be guaranteed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review available evidence, hear affected parties when practical, limit disclosure to what is needed, and explain decisions privately. Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include a correction request, warning, content removal, temporary participation restriction, or permanent exclusion. Severe threats or privacy violations may warrant immediate restriction. Reporters may request reconsideration with new evidence through the same private channel. Retaliation is prohibited.

This is an original project policy. It is not a claim of university oversight or an emergency response service.
